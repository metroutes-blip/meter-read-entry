      About Leela

      This app runs Leela Chess Zero on the iPad itself: Leela's neural network T1-256x10 (a 10-layer transformer, distilled from Leela's large networks) and Leela's own search, with Leela's default settings (PUCT search, cpuct 1.745, policy temperature 1.359, moves-left awareness, two-fold repetitions scored as draws).

      Like the Lc0 engine in your analysis program, Leela doesn't count moves ahead in plies: it explores the moves its network likes most, and reports win / draw / loss chances. Evaluations are shown from White's side; the pawn score uses Lc0's own scale.
